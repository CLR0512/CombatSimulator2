# Once a trial has finished, a trial over the limit is counted as ran over and the target fights on: with Chk's bow
# a Willow Treant's armour keeps its kill time just under the limit, so a few of its fights pass it. Adds one small run.
cs2 run MistyThicket/WillowTreant seed=1
wait 3000
cs2 wait timeout=9
wait 1500
cs2 result
expect reply "MistyThicket/WillowTreant finished"
expect reply "ran-over 3/1000"
cs2 result MistyThicket/WillowTreant full
expect reply "fights: 1000 trials, 3 ran over"
expect reply "ran over 0.3%"
