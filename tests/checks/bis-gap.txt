cs2 gear SiltedOasis/Siltspinner quest=MQ10:done ignore-req pool=every kinds=none seed=1 short wait=0 lock=Helmet,Torso,Ammo,Weapon,Offhand,Amulet,Legs,RingSlot1,Boots

wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
cs2 wait timeout=9
cs2 gear show SiltedOasis/Siltspinner quest=MQ10:done ignore-req pool=every
expect reply "gap: +7.0% [4.4, 9.6] from now"
expect reply "get first: Nightwing Cloak  +3.8% [0.9, 6.8]"
expect reply "Blackstone Gauntlet  +0.8% [−1.8, 3.5]  only with the rest"
# The cloak alone, as a comparison on the same seed and trials: the row's gain.
