# Find best gear for a master: each set fought on every monster of its pool, on task, scored by the master's own
# time-weighted figure; the answer says on how many monsters the set helps and its gain on each. Body, jewellery and
# feet locked, short trials, a slot at a time, in the game's process.
cs2 gear master:Kuren seed=1 trials=30 short wait=0 where=game kinds=none lock=Cape,Amulet,Torso,Legs,RingSlot1,Gloves,Boots
# cs2 wait fails once its 9 s pass with the search still going: sleep first, then one wait.
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
cs2 wait timeout=9
cs2 gear show
expect reply "gear master:Kuren by xp:main, no deaths, margin 1%, seed 1"
expect reply "best: +7.4% [3.8, 11.1] xp:main (11,557 → 12,415 /h)"
expect reply "Helmet: Green Dragonhide Cowl → Cursed Circlet"
expect reply "Weapon: Oak Bow → Toxic Strikes Bow"
expect reply "helps 2 of 10"
expect reply "per target: GolbinForest/Golbin +6.22% [-2.71, 15.15]"
expect reply "hash f562451e8698"
