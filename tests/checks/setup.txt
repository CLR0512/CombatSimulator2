# The checks hold numbers from one character's gear, levels and runs: Chk, kept for them and never played
# (./mm play CombatSimulator2 --load Chk). On any other character they stop here, not on a wrong number.
cs2 setups
expect reply "current  Chk "
# Every check starts on Simulate, in the game's look, with one side and every name shown, once the view is built.
cs2 side b off
cs2 spoilers off
# As teardown leaves it after every check, so the first starts the same way: a player's own experiments left on Chk

cs2 loadouts best clear
cs2 side a ignore-req=off
cs2 side a clear

cs2 unignore all
cs2 filter can-fight kinds=monsters,dungeons,slayer,masters areas=all search=
cs2 skin base
cs2 open simulate
wait 2000
