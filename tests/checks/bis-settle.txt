# Slots the goal can't tell apart are settled by the chance of dying, then damage taken, then food, and every slot
# says what decided it. Both searches climb a slot at a time (short), so the same steps tie every time.
# On Chk's own items against Golbin, which barely hurts it: two items that take less damage for the same XP.
cs2 gear GolbinForest/Golbin pool=own kinds=none seed=1 short wait=0 lock=Helmet,Torso,Ammo,Weapon,Offhand,Legs,Boots
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
wait 9000
cs2 wait timeout=9
cs2 gear show GolbinForest/Golbin kinds=none
expect reply "Cape: Snakeskin Cape → Dire Wolf Cloak  least damage taken (−21.1% [−39.8, −2.4] vs Snakeskin Cape)"
expect reply "Gloves: keep Spider Silk Vambraces  nothing separated them"
expect reply "Weapon: keep Oak Bow  locked"
expect reply "hash 580be4d8a759"
# The settled set opened and run gives the answer's own result.
cs2 gear open
wait 9000
cs2 wait timeout=9
cs2 result
expect reply "results 580be4d8a759"
