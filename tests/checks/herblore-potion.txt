# A combat potion on B goes in the copy's potion slot, given to the copy since Chk's bank holds none, and counts on
# the Herblore page; something that isn't a potion is refused. Against a monster Chk misses often, the Ranged
# potion's max hit and accuracy show in the result and its charges in supplies.
cs2 side b potion=RangedPotionP1A
expect reply "potion=RangedPotionP1A accepted was=none"
wait 2500
cs2 pages b
expect reply "herblore skills changeable changes=1 The potion in your toolbelt. One per side."
cs2 page herblore b
expect reply "RangedPotionP1A 1/1 was=0"
cs2 whatif check potion=Shrimp
expect reply "potion=Shrimp refused not a combat potion"
cs2 whatif DragonsPass/Drake potion=RangedPotionP1A seed=1
cs2 wait timeout=9
cs2 wait timeout=9
wait 1500
cs2 compare show
expect reply "potion=RangedPotionP1A accepted was=none"
expect reply "DragonsPass/Drake  A=12,134 [11,836, 12,432]  B=12,524 [12,281, 12,766]"
expect reply "better"
cs2 result DragonsPass/Drake full
expect reply "used:charges:MelvorBase:RangedPotionP1A"
# None, as the save has it, is no change.
cs2 side b potion=none
expect reply "potion=none no-change was=none"
# Chk owns no potion: the plan says so, not that the build has none.
cs2 gear plan GolbinForest/Golbin kinds=potion
expect reply "potion: 0 to try: you have no combat potions"
cs2 gear plan GolbinForest/Golbin kinds=potion unowned
expect reply "potion: 49 to try"
